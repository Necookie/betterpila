# Data Collection Playbook

**Status:** Draft for review  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-13

## 1. Purpose

This playbook turns public research into reviewable Better Pila records. It applies to web research, public office correspondence, in-person document collection, and community-submitted corrections.

## 2. Before collecting

- Choose one module and a bounded period or list.
- Check whether the record already exists.
- Identify likely primary sources.
- Avoid collecting personal information that is not needed for the public record.
- Confirm whether a file may be stored or should only be linked.

## 3. Intake record

Capture the following before interpretation:

```text
Proposed title/name:
Record type:
Source publisher:
Direct source URL or document identifier:
Source publication date:
Date accessed:
Relevant page/section:
Exact facts supported:
Known limitations or conflicts:
Collector:
Follow-up needed:
```

Do not use a search-results page, AI response, or unsourced social repost as the final citation.

## 4. Module-specific intake

### Officials

- Official name and position.
- Elected or appointed status.
- Term or appointment dates when available.
- Associated office.
- Official public contact only.
- Photo source, permission/license, and attribution.

### Offices and contacts

- Formal office name and responsibilities.
- Physical public-service location.
- Service hours and as-of date.
- Official phone, email, website, and social accounts.
- Whether the number is an emergency hotline.
- Confirmation method and verification date.

### Budgets

- Fiscal year and document type.
- Approval/publication date.
- Currency and accounting basis.
- Exact labels for totals and categories.
- Whether figures are approved, allocated, obligated, disbursed, or spent.
- Source page/table and any calculation performed by Better Pila.

### Projects

- Formal project title and plain-language description.
- Location and implementing office.
- Funding source and public budget.
- Contractor only when documented publicly.
- Start, target, and completion dates.
- Status and status as-of date.
- Evidence for every consequential update.

### Ordinances and resolutions

- Type, number, and legislative year.
- Exact official title.
- Approval and effectivity dates.
- Sponsor/author only when the source identifies one.
- Complete official text or direct link.
- Plain-language summary reviewed against the original.

## 5. Verification

The reviewer checks:

1. The source is authentic and directly supports the claim.
2. Names, numbers, dates, totals, and identifiers match.
3. The record does not combine incompatible reporting periods.
4. The summary preserves important qualifications.
5. Personal details are legitimate public-contact information.
6. Conflicts and missing data are disclosed.
7. File metadata and checksum match the reviewed file.
8. Review and next-review dates are appropriate.

## 6. Contacting a public office

- Identify Better Pila as an independent civic project.
- Ask for public records or clarification precisely and respectfully.
- Keep a record of the office, date, request, and response.
- Do not imply government endorsement or demand unpublished personal data.
- Confirm whether supplied files may be republished or should be linked.

## 7. Bulk imports

- Preserve the original input unchanged in a private working area.
- Map fields explicitly and record transformation rules.
- Validate identifiers, dates, currency, and duplicates.
- Preview changes before writing production data.
- Require normal editorial review after import.
- Record the import batch and allow safe rollback or correction.

## 8. Completion definition

A collected item is not publication-ready until it has a direct citation, normalized fields, a reviewed summary, a verification date, an assigned next review when needed, and approval through the publishing workflow.
